# E-commerce Funnel & Cohort Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes ecommerce customer behavior data to understand purchase conversion, customer retention, repeat purchase behavior, and revenue trends. SQL and Python were used for data cleaning and analysis, while Power BI was used to create interactive dashboards for business insights.

## Tech Stack

* SQL (MySQL)
* Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
* Power BI

## Objectives

* Analyze customer purchase funnel and conversion behavior
* Identify conversion drop-offs across the customer journey
* Perform cohort analysis to study customer retention
* Analyze revenue trends, product performance, and repeat purchases
* Build interactive dashboards for business insights

## Analysis Performed

### Funnel Analysis

* View → Cart → Purchase conversion rates
* Session-based conversion analysis
* Cart abandonment rate analysis

### Cohort Analysis

* Monthly customer retention tracking
* Repeat purchase behavior analysis
* Cohort-based retention trends

### Revenue & Product Analysis

* Monthly revenue trends
* Top-performing product categories
* Brand-level revenue analysis
* Category contribution to total revenue

## Key Insights

* Significant customer drop-off occurred between product views and cart additions.
* Cart-to-purchase conversion remained relatively strong, indicating high purchase intent among cart users.
* Customer retention declined sharply after the first purchase month.
* Revenue was concentrated in computer hardware categories and a limited number of brands.
